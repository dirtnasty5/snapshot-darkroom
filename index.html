How the darkroom works

  1. In game, take photos with SnapShot as usual. With Darkroom crop marks on, SnapShot draws four small pink corner marks around your photo.
  2. Drop the screenshots here. The darkroom finds the marks and cuts out just your photo. The old frame, caption and any UI are left behind.
  3. Your photo gets a brand new polaroid card. Type a caption (fix that typo!), add text, marker doodles, stickers or tape over anything you want hidden.
  4. Download your print. PNG keeps it sharp; the Discord size is easy to share.

No marks? Older screenshots or full-screen shots work too: click Adjust crop and drag the box yourself.

Shortcuts: Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Delete removes the selected item, Ctrl+V pastes a screenshot.

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your edits are remembered on this computer if you drop the same screenshot in again.
